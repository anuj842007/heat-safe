Maharashtra has a heat rule. HeatSetu shows who follows it.

The state SOP moves outdoor work to 6–11 AM and 4–8 PM and stops it from 12 to 4 PM on orange and red alert days. Workers who rest lose a day's pay, and officers cannot see which sites comply. HeatSetu gives each site its own risk, gives workers a safe way to rest, and gives officers proof.

I am a worker or supervisor

Risk for my site, map, alerts, voice in Marathi, water and rest timers, 108 help, report a problem, record a break.

I am an officer

Risk map, compliance, repeat offenders, complaints, Heat-Safe ratings, pay ledger, notices and reports.

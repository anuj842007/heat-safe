HeatSetu

A heat safety prototype for outdoor workers, made for the Seva First Innovation Challenge (Sector 11, option 3).

To run it, open this folder in VS Code, right-click index.html and choose Open with Live Server. Weather, map tiles and fonts need an internet connection.

index.html is the overview, worker.html is for workers and supervisors, and officer.html is for officers.

Weather comes from Open-Meteo. IMD alerts, site records, complaints, water points and pay are sample data, and pay is simulated. Records are saved in the browser only. The risk cutoffs are our own demo values.
